Caseking Media & Brand Strategy

From performance marketing to brand-led growth.

Not replacing performance, but rebalancing it.

  • Retailer: drive traffic and sales
  • Brand: build authority
  • Distribution: support partner brands
See the context See the media model

01 - Context

Three roles: Retailer, Brand, Distribution.

These roles create tension if they are not clearly structured.

Retailer

Drive traffic and sales

Brand

Build authority

Distribution

Support partner brands

Current state

  • Strong performance marketing across Google Search and PMax
  • Growing paid social on Meta
  • SEO + Direct account for approx. 45% of traffic

Which means

Strong at capturing demand.

Underdeveloped in shaping and creating demand.

02 - Strategic Shift

Where we come from, and where we are going.

Effective short-term performance needs stronger long-term brand ownership.

Where we come from

  • Performance marketing focused on sales
  • Distribution role focused on partner brands

The result

  • Strong business
  • Limited Caseking brand ownership
  • High dependency on paid channels
  • External brands dominate visibility

Before

  • Product-first communication
  • Channel-driven execution
  • Short-term optimization

After

  • System-first communication
  • Intent-driven media
  • Long-term brand building

What we are building

Caseking brand equity

Getting it right Reliable performance No guesswork

03 - Core Principle

Separate intent, not channels.

We do not assign fixed roles like Google equals performance or Meta equals branding.

Do not do this

  • Google = performance
  • Meta = branding

Do this instead

  • Use every channel against user intent
  • Match message depth to buying stage

04 - Intent-Based Media Model

High intent captures demand. Mid intent shapes decisions. Low intent creates demand.

The model is built around what the user needs next.

High Intent

Capture demand

  • Users are ready to buy
  • Channels: Google Search, Google PMax
  • Role: capture demand and drive conversion

Mid Intent

Shape decisions

  • Users are comparing options
  • Channels: Meta conversion, retargeting, YouTube, Display
  • Role: reduce uncertainty and guide decisions

Low Intent

Create demand

  • Users are not actively shopping
  • Channels: Meta paid social, YouTube, Influencers
  • Role: build brand and generate demand

05 - Communication Principle

We communicate outcomes, not products.

Shift from specs-first marketing to system marketing.

Not

  • Specs first
  • Features first

But

  • What it does
  • How it performs
  • Why it works

Shift to system marketing.

06 - Brand Vs Partner Balance

Caseking must stay the lead voice.

Partner presence supports the system, not the other way around.

Caseking-led communication

70-80%

  • Systems
  • Performance
  • Outcomes

Partner-supported communication

20-30%

  • Launches
  • Co-branded systems
  • Product highlights

Never do

  • Brand-first campaigns
  • Standalone supplier messaging
  • Traffic to brand-only pages

Protects Caseking brand authority.

07 - Channel Roles

Each channel has a clear job inside the system.

Intent decides the role, not the platform label.

Google Search + PMax

Demand capture

High-intent queries and system-focused keywords.

Meta Paid Social

Demand creation + decision shaping

System storytelling and outcome-driven messaging.

YouTube

Proof and education

Performance demos, comparisons, and why-it-works content.

SEO + Direct

Authority and long-term demand

Guides, setup recommendations, and educational content.

Store (Repositioned)

Owned media asset

Content engine, proof environment, and partner activation platform.

13 - KPI Framework

Performance, brand, and store all need clear measurement.

The strategy needs commercial and brand proof at the same time.

Performance KPIs

  • ROAS
  • Conversion rate
  • CAC

Brand KPIs

  • Direct traffic
  • Branded search
  • Engagement

Store KPIs

  • Content output
  • Content performance
  • Engagement

Strategic KPI: percent of system-driven revenue.

14 - Success Criteria

The strategy works when brand and revenue improve together.

Success is visible in demand creation, confidence, and brand ownership.

  • Demand is created, not only captured
  • Caseking becomes associated with getting it right
  • Conversion improves through confidence
  • Brand equity grows alongside revenue

Final Principle

We don't sell performance. We prove it.

This setup will work. Properly.
